## Game format

Math Race Live is a synchronized, skill-based arithmetic game. Players in a match receive the same server-generated questions. Current matches contain five questions within the displayed global gameplay period, followed by a results period. Questions may use addition, subtraction, multiplication, or exact integer division.

The backend is authoritative for match phase, question delivery, accepted answers, validated response time, correctness, points, final results, ranks, and leaderboards. Client displays and network timing do not override the backend record.

Wrong, invalid, duplicate, out-of-sequence, or late answers may receive no points or be rejected. Network interruption can prevent an answer from reaching the server before the applicable deadline.

Match rankings and the daily leaderboard are calculated from authoritative records. Equal scores may share a competition rank under the implemented ranking rules. Results may be delayed briefly while accepted answers are finalized.

In the current version of Math Race Live, points, ranks, and leaderboard positions are game records only.
